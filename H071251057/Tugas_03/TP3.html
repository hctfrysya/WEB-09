Who is Adéla?

Adéla Jergová is a Slovak singer-songwriter and dancer known for her captivating presence and artistic expression. With a passion for music, dance, and performance, she continues to explore her creativity and share her unique style.

Through her work, Adéla brings together music and movement, creating a distinctive identity that reflects her personality, energy, and dedication to her craft.
